# On Call Policy

**Agent-trigger:** Running the rotation: shift length, escalation paths, handoff expectations, compensation, and what an on-call engineer is and is not responsible for. Load when setting up or changing a rotation, when an escalation is missed, and when deciding whether something deserves to page a human at night.

Shifts are one week and no engineer takes two consecutive weeks. The primary acknowledges within five
minutes; unacknowledged pages escalate to the secondary and then to the engineering manager.

A page at night must be actionable by the person receiving it. If the runbook says to wait for morning,
the alert should not have paged.

Handoff is a written summary of what is still burning, not a verbal "all quiet". The outgoing engineer
files follow-up work before the shift ends.
